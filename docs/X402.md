# x402 on NEAR in Bloqvia — the market desk and the purses (Nov 13, 2026)

HTTP 402 "Payment Required" turned into a payment handshake: a client asks for a resource, the server answers
`402` with a price, the client's wallet signs a payment, retries with the proof, a *facilitator* verifies and settles
it on chain, and the resource comes back. No account with the seller, no API key, no invoice. On NEAR the payment is
a NEP-141 `ft_transfer` signed as a NEP-366 delegate action, so the payer needs no NEAR for gas: the facilitator
sponsors the outer transaction, exactly as Bloqvia's relayer sponsors every learner's call. Library:
`@fastnear/x402` (x402 v2, scheme `exact`, `near:testnet`).

## What Bloqvia sells and who buys

| Route | Price | Sold to | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /desk/signal` | 0.01 wNEAR | the three NPC traders, from their purses | Bloq's prices now, the day's average per item, the price-call round, the other traders' last moves — read from the chain and the relayer's own snapshots |
| `GET /passport/proof?accountId=` | 0.05 wNEAR | anyone with a purse (a dapp, a DAO, an airdrop) | a signed statement of one Passport as the chain holds it (`graduated`, `level`, `stamps`, `quests_completed`); signed with the relayer's key, which is an access key of `bloqvia.testnet` on chain, so it verifies without asking us |

The desk is `relayer/src/desk.ts`: the x402 resource server over our `node:http` routes, with the facilitator running
in the same process on the relayer's own account. The purse is `relayer/src/purse.ts`.

## The cage, again

The traders already play inside `allowedMoves`: the model picks a move only from the list the rules allow. The purse
is the same shape for money. Before a signature exists the purse checks the 402's own numbers against rules the model
cannot touch: the host must be on its list (the desk's own address), the token must be wrapped NEAR, the price at most
0.01 wNEAR a call, the day's total at most 0.2 wNEAR; and the purse is a sub-account holding a day's budget, so the
balance is a ceiling no rule can lift. The model decides *whether a signal is worth buying* (the bull every look, the
bear on a dip, the patient one twice an hour — `wantsSignal`); the purse decides *whether the payment is allowed*.
A refusal is shown on `/market` like a held move: `the purse refused the signal (budget: 0.01 wNEAR asked, 0 of today's
0.2 wNEAR left)`.

No payment ever reaches a reward, a price or another player's balance. A purse only buys answers; the desk only sells
what the chain already holds.

## Why wrapped NEAR, why our own facilitator

x402 on NEAR pays in NEP-141 tokens, not native NEAR. USDC testnet would have meant a faucet on a deadline; `wrap.testnet`
we can mint ourselves (`near_deposit`) and it is a real NEP-141 token with storage registration like any other. The
library ships a self-hosted facilitator that composes in-process with the resource server, so the relayer verifies and
settles its own sales: no third party, no API key, and the trust is the one a learner already places in the account
that pays their gas. Both choices are stated as limits in the README.

## Accounts

`purse-patient.bloqvia.testnet`, `purse-bull.bloqvia.testnet`, `purse-bear.bloqvia.testnet`: sub-accounts of the
relayer, made once by `npm run x402:setup -- --apply` (scripts/), each with one full-access key (x402's signer needs
one; a function-call key cannot authorise a token transfer), 0.3 NEAR, registered on `wrap.testnet`, 0.2 NEAR wrapped.
Their keys live in `relayer/.env` and Railway's variables as `PURSE_*`, `PURSE2_*`, `PURSE3_*`; never in git.
`bloqvia.testnet` is the payee and the facilitator's gas sponsor, registered on `wrap.testnet` by the same script.

## Seeing it

- `curl -i https://relay.bloqvia.app/desk/signal` → the 402 and the `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` header.
- `curl -s https://relay.bloqvia.app/market | jq '.desk, [.traders[] | {temper, purse, signal}]'` → sales and each
  trader's last purchase or refusal, with transaction hashes.
- `npm run desk:buy -- --relayer https://relay.bloqvia.app --proof <account>` (with a purse in the env) → the whole
  round trip in the open, both routes, with explorer links and the proof verified locally.
- `npm run attack` attempts 10–12 → the purse refuses an unknown host and a price over its cap; the desk refuses a
  garbage payment header.
