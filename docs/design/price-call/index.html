The price call — mock

Under the curve on every trade card: one question on Bloq's price in ten minutes, two buttons, a stake, and what the crowd thinks. Four states of the same block, left to right: open and nobody called yet; your call placed; the result (won, lost, unmoved); the window closed.

What changes

1A Price call block under "He pays · 24 h" on each trade card. Headline question, More / Less, a stake of 10 to 50 gold (default 20), the clock of the round.

2The crowd line: a thin bar and "62 % of the gold says more · 3 calls" — the pool as the crowd's expectation. Hidden until someone calls.

3After your call: "Your call: more, from 44 · settles in 6:12". After settlement: "You called it: +18 gold", "Not this time: −20 gold" or "Price didn't move: 20 gold back", with the close price, shown once.

4In the last three minutes of a round calls close: "Calls open again in 2:50"; the buttons are disabled, the crowd line stays.

The contract decides everything (`calls.rs`): one call per item per round, stakes 10–50, settlement by the price rule, winners split the losers' stakes. No house money. The relayer settles a minute after the round ends.
