1

Scroll bar on phones and tablets

iPhone and Android browsers ignore scroll-bar colours: they always draw their own grey bar, which fades in while you scroll. The only way to get the lime one is to hide theirs and draw our own. It's a thin lime bar that appears while you scroll, fades out about a second after you stop, and can be dragged. Scroll the phone below.

3

Quest window: drag the grey bar down to close it

The grey bar now works like an iPhone sheet. Drag it down more than about a third of the way, or flick it, and the window closes. Let go sooner and it springs back. Only the bar starts the drag, so scrolling the quest text still scrolls. The same goes for the Ask Bloq window.

4

Bloq stays with you on the website's card sections

On a phone the cards stack in one column, so Bloq stays at the top of the section and you lose him when you scroll down. Three ways to keep him with you. Each phone below holds “A little context” (4 cards) and “Your first adventure” (3 chapters, where he changes hat). Scroll each one.

A · guide in the corner

He waits in the bottom-left corner

While a card section is on screen, he sits in the corner and talks about the card in the middle of the screen, changing hats on the chapters. He leaves when the section ends.

B · hops card to card

He hops down onto each card

As you scroll, he jumps to the card in view and sits on its top edge, then talks about it (and changes hat on the chapters). It's the closest to how he sits on cards on desktop.

C · swipe the cards

The cards swipe sideways under him

The cards become a sideways row. When you swipe, he walks side to side along the top, as on desktop, then sits and talks. It keeps him in view, but swiping is less obvious than scrolling.
