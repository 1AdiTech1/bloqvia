Seasons on the map: cards, walking, waiting, jumping

This is the real map component. It opens with Season 1 done: Bloq is sitting on the Season 2 card's edge, waiting just left of the Start button, like on the "Start without a wallet" card. Press Start Season 2 ↓ and he jumps over the card onto Digital Ownership. ▶ Walk Season 1 from the start shows him walking the road and round every block. ▶ Finish quest 3 shows the last stretch, the cheer at the shop, and his walk down to the card, where he sits on the edge.

1 · Season cardsOne slim card per season, two lines, both naming the season's three blocks: "Season 1 · The Block Fields · 3/3" over "The Vault · The Fuel System · Smart Agreements", and "Season 2 · Network City" over "Digital ownership · Transactions · Decentralised finance". No block number. Phones get the short line ("Vault · Fuel · Agreements", "Ownership · Tx · DeFi").
2 · Walking, never hoppingHe walks the road facing where he's going, leaves it just before each block and walks round it to his spot beside it (left of The Fuel System, right of Smart Agreements), then walks back onto the road after it. He walks round the shop's left side to the door. No hops anywhere, phones included.
4 · Walking to the card, sitting on its edgeAfter the cheer at the shop he walks down the left of the shop (clear of its text), steps onto the Season 2 card's top edge and walks along it to just left of the Start button. Then he sits down on the edge exactly as on the "Start without a wallet" card: he lowers by a fifth of his height in 420ms, with only his shoes over the front. Once seated he faces right, laughs, his eyes follow your pointer, and tapping him gives the same little bounce. "Ready for Season 2? Press Start and I'll jump in!" The only jump is when you press Start.
