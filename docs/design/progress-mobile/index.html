  1. On the cards: he sits on the top edge of the reward card in the middle of the screen, holds that reward (coin, key, fuel cell, seal) and says its line. Moving to the next card is a hop, as in the other sections.
  2. At the Passport: when the panel reaches the middle of the screen, he takes a bigger jump down into it and lands next to the card, a little bigger and standing. He cheers, sparks fly, the card straightens up, and the lime seal stamps onto it. Then he says “Graduate and your Passport is sealed for good. That's the one you can't buy.”
