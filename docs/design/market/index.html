The market never sleeps — mock

Bloq's shop with three additions: a price history on every trade card, the NPC trader's last move in the stall, and a fast-forward button that runs a day of trading on testnet in front of a judge, in about two minutes. Press the button in either frame to see the curves move.

What changes

1Each trade card shows He pays · last 24 h as a small curve from the relayer's price snapshots, with the change since yesterday.

2The stall gets a Market · live strip: what the NPC trader (an account like a learner's, trader.bloqvia.testnet) last did, and when. The trader follows three readable rules; prices are still the contract's.

3Watch a day of trading: six scripted learners sign up, play the quests and trade for about two minutes; the curves and the treasury move live. Once every 10 minutes; the progress bar shows the rounds.

Nothing in the contracts changes. The trader and the bots go through the relayer exactly like a player (NEP-366), so every trade is on the explorer.
