Season 2 ends at the Passport office

Season 1 ends at Bloq's shop. Season 2's road, below the gate, ends at the Passport office (passport.bloqvia.testnet on the plate), where Bloq the officer stamps your Passport. The map below is the real component: press ▶ Finish chapter 06 and watch Bloq walk to the office, the stamp land and both Bloqs cheer.

1

The road and the stamp moment

There is no extra button: the right answer to the third chapter is the transaction that stamps the Passport (the core contract asks the passport contract in the same transaction). Back on the map, Bloq walks to the office, your Passport pops up over it, the stamp slams down, and both Bloqs cheer. Afterwards the door says "✓ Stamped on NEAR · Season 2" and links to your Passport and to that transaction.

The stamped Passport

The same card as today, with the Season 2 stamp across it and "Seasons 1 · 2" in the fields. On the Passport page there's a row per season, and the stamp's transaction opens on the explorer.

Under the hood (after your "go")

  1. core: chapter- ids count as bonus quests: they pay BloqGold and an item and never change the Passport level. They are refused until Season 1's three quests are done. When the last of the three chapters is completed, complete_lesson asks the passport contract for the stamp in the same transaction. That way every player's existing key works (a new method would not be on their key's allow-list). resync_stamp(account_id) repeats the request if a stamp ever failed to land; anyone can call it, and it still checks the three chapters.
  2. passport: a new add_stamp that only core can call. It adds "season-2" once, so a second call changes nothing. The stamp shows in the token's on-chain metadata (extra). Stamps are kept in their own storage map, so the contract needs no migration and existing Passports read exactly as before.
  3. Tests: core 36 (chapters pay but don't level; chapters wait for Season 1; the third chapter stamps; a daily quest never stamps; resync refused early and allowed after), passport 11 (core stamps once and it survives level changes; the owner and the learner can't stamp; no stamp without a Passport; a Passport without stamps reads as before).
  4. Then: two code-only redeploys and the chapter seed (your go-ahead), and one Lovable prompt for the seasons map, the stamp moment, the Passport card and page, the Reveal buttons and the landing line.
