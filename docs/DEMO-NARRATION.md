# Demo narration — the spoken lines only (for ElevenLabs or a phone recording)

Read at a calm pace, as if showing a friend. About 400 words, 3 minutes. Each block is one beat of the
video; the time in brackets is how long that block of audio should last, give or take a few seconds,
so the picture can keep up. Leave a one-second pause between blocks. Deliver as one MP3 or WAV at
44.1 kHz (one file is easiest to lay over the picture; separate files per block are fine too, named 01
to 09). In ElevenLabs: a male US voice, "Narration" or "Conversational" style, stability around 50,
speed normal, no music.

---

**01 · Opening (12 s)**

Most people quit Web3 at the wallet: a seed phrase, gas, a first transaction they don't understand.
Bloqvia is a game that teaches it by playing it, for real, on NEAR testnet, with none of that in the
way. Every move you'll see is a real transaction, and I'll show the chain each time.

**02 · The account (18 s)**

That was a NEAR account being created and a Passport NFT minted to it. NEAR lets a relayer pay the
gas for a transaction my account signs, so the key stays in this browser and I never buy anything.
Here's the account, here's the block.

**03 · First quest, first transaction (22 s)**

The answer is checked by the contract, not by the app: pick a wrong one and the chain refuses it. The
reward is written by the contract too. Here's the transaction: my account signed it, the relayer paid
the gas.

**04 · The AI quest (20 s)**

Today's quest was written this morning by an AI agent on NEAR AI Cloud, running in a trusted enclave:
it's signed, and anyone can verify the signature. The model writes text. It never touches a reward, a
price or a balance; the contract does that.

**05 · The living market (20 s)**

This is a market, and it never sleeps. Prices come from a rule in the contract: the more Bloq holds,
the less he pays, and every hour he ships part of his surplus so prices come back. Bots and three NPC
traders keep trading while you're away, so the curve is a day of real trades, all on chain. I just
sold two, and his price fell.

**06 · The traders think (14 s)**

And the traders are AI agents: patient, bull, bear. Each reads the prices, picks a move and says why,
signed. But it can only pick from the moves its rules allow; anything else is overruled. Agents with
wallets, inside a cage they can't open.

**07 · The price call (16 s)**

And every price is a question. I'm staking twenty gold that in ten minutes Bloq pays the same. The
contract remembers today's price, and when the round ends it reads its own rule and pays the side
that was right from the stakes of the sides that weren't. No house, no odds: a market on the market.

**08 · Graduate, claim, vote (30 s)**

Two more quests and the Passport is ready.

Graduating sealed the Passport, and the Passport is a seat: this account now votes in the cohort
DAO, a Sputnik DAO on NEAR. And a bounty board pays for what the chain can verify. I claim "first
graduate"; the board reads my record from the game contract and pays. No one approved it; no model
was asked.

**09 · Founders, and the close (20 s)**

Finish Season 2 and you're a founder: you propose what the cohort pays for, founders vote with their
own keys, and the DAO itself adds the bounty to the board. Learn, earn a seat, govern.

Bloqvia. Open source, on NEAR testnet, every step in the README. Thanks.
