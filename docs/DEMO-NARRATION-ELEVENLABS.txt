This is a market, and it never sleeps. Prices come from a rule in the contract: the more Block holds, the less he pays, and every hour he ships part of his surplus so prices come back. Bots and three NPC traders keep trading while you're away, so the curve is a day of real trades, all on chain. I just sold two, and his price fell. <break time="1.5s" />

And the traders are AI agents: patient, bull, bear. Each reads the prices, picks a move and says why, signed. But it can only pick from the moves its rules allow; anything else is overruled. Agents with wallets, inside a cage they can't open. <break time="1.5s" />

And every price is a question. I'm staking twenty gold that in ten minutes Block pays the same. The contract remembers today's price, and when the round ends it reads its own rule and pays the side that was right from the stakes of the sides that weren't. No house, no odds: a market on the market. <break time="1.5s" />
