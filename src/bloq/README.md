# `src/bloq/` — Bloq, the animated character

Owned by Adi (like `src/near/`). Screens import from `@/bloq`; they do not edit these files.
The drawing and motion were approved in the design previews (`docs/design/landing-v2/`, `docs/design/game-v2/`);
`engine.ts` is a port of that preview's `bloq-rig.js`. Since Nov 9 motion changes are tried first in the Motion Lab
(`docs/design/motion-lab/`, which runs copies of this folder side by side), approved there, then ported here; the
rules they follow are in `docs/MOTION-RULES.md`.

| Export | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Bloq` | The character. Props: `hat`, `prop`, `pose`, `outfit`, `facing`, `floating`, `head`, `className`, `label`. Ref (`BloqHandle`): `jump(kind?)` (`"small"` keeps his pose, `"happy"` the default cheers, `"big"` is a celebration with a tuck), `talk(ms, text?)`, `setMood(m, ms)`, `look(dx, dy)`, `aimAt(el)`, `walking(on, follow?)`, `wobble()`, `rideWave(on)`, `element()`. Size it with the wrapper's width (it keeps a 3:4 box). Motion (Nov 9, `docs/MOTION-RULES.md`): changing `facing` plays a turn (a squash-swap, head first, never thinner than 62%, 300 ms); `walking(true)` starts with a lean and a dip, `walking(false)` ends with a settle (400 ms; a new pose, a jump or pointing skips to the settle); with `follow` the steps follow the distance in 2D on the page; `talk(ms, text)` moves the mouth with the words (shapes, pauses, nods, brows on questions, one gesture a sentence, blinks), `talk(ms)` alone flaps as before. Phase 2 (Nov 9): hops follow gravity; a keyed walk; three cheers that never repeat back to back; sitting down and standing up with a body; idle beats every 4–8 s (a glance, a weight shift, after 10 s the hat or a stretch); props have weight (they trail the hand, medium ones ride lower, the cube takes two hands) and walking with one he carries it up in front; mood `"o"` plays a surprise take; the laugh shakes; the wave has a loose wrist. |
| `SpeechBubble`, `useSay`, `talkMs` | Glass speech bubble that sits above Bloq (or on his left with `side="left"`), stays on screen, `tone="light"` on navy sections. `side="right"`: a sibling after Bloq in a row, in the flow at every width, tail pointing back at him (Passport page, shop). Every line Bloq says in the app uses this bubble. The tail is always at his head: a tail on the bubble's bottom edge slides along it to sit under his head wherever the bubble is (nudged in from a screen edge, or moved to his left by a page's CSS), and the nudge happens at once so the bubble and its tail never drift apart (`docs/design/tail-rule/`, Nov 9). `useSay()` gives `[line, say(text)]`; call `bloq.talk(talkMs(text), text)` with it (with the text his mouth follows the words). Put the bubble in the same `relative` box as the Bloq. |
| `leap` | A real jump for a Bloq whose box moves with `transform: translate(x, y)` and `width`: `leap(hostEl, { x, y, w }, { body, lift?, onTakeoff(dir), onLand })` crouches, flies an arc with gravity (0.52–1 s by distance), leans, lands with a squash and a puff of dust; returns `cancel()`. `body` wraps only the `<Bloq/>`. Reduced motion: straight to the spot. Used by the landing's Progress section on phones (Bloq into the Passport panel). Design: `docs/design/hop-jump/` (option A). |
| `walkTo` | Bloq walks or runs along the ground like a person: `walkTo(hostEl, x, { y?, onArrive })` speeds up, keeps a steady pace and slows down before he stops; up to 3 body widths is a calm walk, further is a run; `run: false` keeps it a calm walk however far (along a card's edge). Returns `stop()`, which leaves him where he is and returns that x. Pair it with `bloq.walking(true, true)`: with `follow` his steps follow how far he really moves (no sliding) and fast trips turn into a run. Used by the desktop landing's "A little context" section and the after-seasons tour on desktop (streak card → Today's quest). Design: `docs/design/fixes-n8/` (option B), `docs/design/morning-fixes/` (section 2). |
| `FloatingCubes` | Six faded cubes behind the hero Bloq, each with its own random motion; ref `hop()`. Place inside the hero's square stage. |
| `BlockSea` | The block sea for the surfing Bloq; pass `riderRef` (the Bloq's wrapper) and it lifts him onto the crest each frame. The rider fires a `bloq:splash` DOM event (`detail.dir`) when he hops back onto the board. |
| `BloqviaLogo` | The logo in motion: blocks drop and stack, the name types itself. Colours `#D4F033 / #A6C21A`, name Space Grotesk 600. |
| `BloqCoin` | The BloqGold coin (lime disc, inner ring, the Bloqvia block mark), the same coin Bloq holds. `size` in px; `label` makes it announced. Use it everywhere BloqGold is drawn. |
| `BloqFace` | Only Bloq's head with his hat, cut under the chin (box height = size × 0.685, cut under the chin; the hat may stick out above it), for dialog corners and card headers. Eyes follow the pointer; laughs on hover; ref `jump()`, `talk(ms)`. Sit its bottom edge on a card's top edge so he peeks over it. |
| `AgentPod` | Bloq the AI agent in a round window he leans out of (hover, focus of the button around it, or tap). `size` in px (32 inline, 52 sheet header, 58 dock); `lean={false}` keeps him inside a plain round avatar (for headers inside a box that clips). Ref (`AgentPodHandle`): `peek(ms)`, `jump()`, `talk(ms)`. Decorative: label the button that holds it. |
| `motionOff`, `setMotion`, `useMotionOff`, `useMotionRoot` | Motion on or off for the whole site and the game (the landing's Motion switch). The choice is kept on this device (`localStorage` key `bloqvia:motion`); until the player picks, it follows the device's `prefers-reduced-motion`. Off: `<body>` gets `still`, every animation ends at once and transitions are skipped (`bloq.css`), Bloq stands still, `leap` / `walkTo` place him at once. `useMotionRoot()` once at the app's root (returns `off`, for `<MotionConfig reducedMotion={off ? "always" : "never"}>`); `useMotionOff()` anywhere in React; `setMotion(on)` is the switch; `motionOff()` outside React. Design: `docs/design/morning-fixes/` (section 3, option A). |
| `followCamera`, `keepInView`, `scrollBox` | The game camera: the screen stays on Bloq. `const stop = followCamera(el)` keeps him in the middle band of the screen (30–68%) while he moves, gliding only when he nears an edge; the player scrolling lets it go. `keepInView(el)` is the same in one step, at once. Works with the page's scroll or the nearest scrolling box. Design: `docs/design/morning-fixes/` (section 1). |
| `ChainTrail` | The game map as a NEAR world: isometric floor, validator towers, chunks drifting up, a road of block tiles that lights up lime with lamps and moving transactions (it runs from the side of one block to the side of the next, fades out before each block and carries on into it as three stepping stones that turn lime once reached; `docs/design/roads/`, F), at the foot of the map an orchard of Merkle trees in Season 1 (`docs/design/fields-scenery/`, C) and a skyline in Season 2, three quest blocks drawn as a vault / battery station / contract machine, Bloq the guide (cap) at the current stop and Bloq the merchant (top hat) behind the shop door. Props: `stops` (3 × `{ id, title, kind: "vault" \| "battery" \| "contract", post, reward? }`), `level` (0–3; raise it after the quest sheet closes and he walks there at a walking pace (speeds up, slows down on bends and where he turns, steps on the ground), settles, then hops and speaks, with the screen following him: `followCamera`; with motion off he is placed there at once and the screen jumps to him), `onPlay(i)`, `doorAccount`, `doorOpen?`, `door` (content under the door), `blockHeight` (pass `useFinalBlock()` from `@/near`; null hides the number), `tip?`, `intro?`. Ref: `say(text)`. Design: `docs/design/game-v2/`. |

**Values.** Hats: `cap cap2 bucket beanie captain hardhat beret detective agent moto astro tophat aviator surf grad`.
Props: `star chip thumb key fuel scroll bulb cube magnifier passport badge coin`.
Poses: `idle thumbs wave point hold present cheer walk sit ride bike space board boardwave`.
Outfits: `surf agent astro`.

**Rules.** Never the same hat on two Bloqs of one page (make a v2 hat instead). The AI agent always wears `agent` and appears only through `AgentPod`. Fuel / gas is always drawn as a battery (lucide `BatteryCharging`), never a flame or a pump. Motion off (the switch, or the device asking for less motion): everything
stands still and stays visible. SSR-safe: nothing touches the DOM until mount.
